Walk around it

One cabinet. Every side considered.

Use the arrows to walk around the Sunny NEX-G. Each face carries its own console and three-nozzle bay, so two vehicles can fill at once.

  • 01
    Front-left

    Louvred side panel keeps the hydraulics ventilated. Hoses hang from overhead swivels so they never touch the ground.

  • 02
    Front

    A console at eye height and a colour-coded nozzle bay for petrol, diesel and premium.

  • 03
    Front-right

    A slim footprint that fits standard islands. Lower service doors give quick access to the meters.

  • 04
    Left side

    A clean profile with nozzles on both faces, so lanes on either side of the island get served.

  • 05
    Right side

    Vented lower cabinet, mains entry and lockable service access, all kept clear of the forecourt.

  • 06
    Elevated three-quarter

    Double-sided: the reverse face mirrors this one with its own console and three more nozzles, for six in total.

Front-left
The console

Everything the attendant needs. Nothing they don't.

Tap a marker to see what each part of the face does.

01Emergency stop

A high-visibility mushroom switch cuts power to the dispenser instantly.

02Main display

A large readout shows amount (₹), volume (L), unit price and density, with "ensure zero before delivery" printed beside it.

03Per-grade price

A separate ₹/L display for each grade, so customers always see what they pay.

04Safety signage

No smoking, no mobile phones, no open flame, printed directly on the face.

05Receipt printer

A built-in thermal printer with a status light issues a slip for every sale.

06Preset keypad

Sealed metal keys with ₹ and litre presets plus F1–F4 function keys.

Up close

Details that last on a busy forecourt.

  1. 01
    Colour-coded grades

    Blue, green and yellow nozzles make each grade clear at a glance and help prevent misfuelling.

  2. 02
    Ergonomic nozzles

    Moulded, embossed boots that sit comfortably in the hand through a long shift.

  3. 03
    Overhead swivels

    Hoses drop from above and rotate freely, which cuts kinking and wear.

  4. 04
    Secure holsters

    Deep holsters seat each nozzle firmly, with a drip tray below.

Heritage

More than a century of putting fuel in tanks.

Tatsuno India is a subsidiary of Tatsuno Corporation, Tokyo, a global manufacturer of equipment for petroleum retail.

1911

Tatsuno Corporation is founded in Tokyo.

1919

It builds Japan's first petrol dispensing pump.

2012

The Indian manufacturing plant is commissioned, Tatsuno's largest investment outside Japan.

Today

Sunny NEX-G dispensers built in Taloja for India's oil marketing companies.
